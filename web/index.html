02 / Live Cockpit

Interrogation in progress.

A 3-turn session. The subject reads a scenario, answers aloud, and VERITY simultaneously analyzes their words, voice, face and pulse — producing a credibility verdict for each turn and the session as a whole.

03 / Methodology

Four streams.
One verdict.

No single signal is sufficient. Deception leaks across modalities — a hedge in speech, a brow-tightening, an elevated pulse, a hesitation. VERITY captures all four and lets the model weigh them against each other.

M-01

Linguistic.

Hedging, denial, distancing language, repetition, sentence specificity, sentiment balance. 15 features derived from the transcript on every utterance.

15 text features · hedge / denial / distancing ratios · Ott et al. 2013 lexicon
M-02

Vocal.

Web Audio captures cadence, pause duration, speech rate, and energy envelope. The Web Speech API transcribes in real time and feeds the linguistic stream.

· cadence & pause structure · energy envelope (RMS) · live speech-to-text
M-03

Micro-facial.

MediaPipe FaceLandmarker reads 478 points at 30Hz. From these we estimate 4 of Ekman's Action Units — AU1, AU4, AU7, AU12 — plus blink rate.

· AU1 / AU4 / AU7 / AU12 · blink rate per minute · Ekman FACS reference
M-04

Cardiac.

Remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) extracts the green-channel pulse from a forehead ROI. We derive heart rate and HRV without a single sensor on the subject.

· HR mean (bpm) · HRV (ms) · contactless · webcam only

Disclosure · Known limits V1 caveat

100% accuracy is an artifact of clean, well-separated synthetic data. In V1 the physiological features were generated against published distributions from polygraph and FACS literature, not measured.

The V2 roadmap collects real interrogation data with measured sensor input; expected performance drops to a realistic ~72–80% text-only, ~85–91% multimodal, consistent with current deception-detection literature.
